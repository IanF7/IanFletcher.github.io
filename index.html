Featured Project

Pokémon Copper

Solo developer · Pokémon Essentials (RPG Maker XP) · Released 2026

A full-length, open-world Pokémon fan game set in the Azori Region, a region inspired by Arizona. I designed and built it over three years as the solo developer, with sprite art help from one collaborator.

  • Designed 113 original Pokémon, 10 regional variants, 22 Mega Evolutions, 47 moves, and 10 abilities, balanced within a 500-species regional Pokédex
  • Built open-world progression: after the first gym, players choose their own route, and trainers and wild Pokémon scale with badge count and party level
  • Added player-friendly systems: an optional level cap, toggleable EXP All, autosave, and PokéSummons that replace HMs
  • Grew a 600+ member Discord community; the launch trailer reached 33,000 views on a brand-new channel
  • Supporting the game after launch with bug-fix patches, a modded Pokémon Showdown server, and a post-game update (Battle Frontier with 5 facilities) coming December 2026
